## Welcome to the mess

**Bad Sewing** is a scrappy, honest look at learning to sew without pretending every seam
is Pinterest-perfect. Expect crooked hems, salvaged fabric, hard-won tips, and the
occasional triumphant finish that actually fits.

No perfectionism. No matching thread. Just real projects, real mistakes, and the
stubborn joy of making things with your hands.
